C-Four Electric Window Winder · six videos for review

Leaving notes is easy:

  1. Pause a video wherever something should change, then type your note beside it. The moment and a picture of that frame are saved with your note.
  2. Want to point at something? Press Draw and circle it on the paused frame.
  3. Press Save (or Ctrl/⌘ + Enter). Notes are saved on this page, and clicking a note's time jumps the video there.

These are preview renders: the final full-HD versions will show fine details (button labels, small product markings) more sharply. Known issues are listed under each video. You can edit or delete your own notes.
